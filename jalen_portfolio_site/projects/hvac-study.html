The challenge

The initial comparison approach did not fully account for outside-air percentage, which materially affected whether the configurations could be compared fairly.

My approach

I restructured the analysis around outside-air requirements, emissions rates, equipment configurations, operating assumptions, and regional or facility conditions rather than relying on tonnage alone.

Engineering & iteration

Supplier feedback changed the original PDCA direction, so the analysis was revised rather than defended. That meant revisiting assumptions, reorganizing the comparison logic, and identifying the additional operating inputs needed to convert emissions rates into useful annualized values.

Result

The study became a better engineering decision framework because it reflected how the equipment would actually be applied instead of only comparing nominal capacities.
