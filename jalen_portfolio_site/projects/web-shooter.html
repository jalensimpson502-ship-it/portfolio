Wearable Web-Shooter Prototype

A compact wearable prototype combining an ESP32 controller, microswitch input, servo actuation, wiring, packaging, and iterative testing.

The challenge

The device needed to package actuation, control input, power, and wiring into a wearable form while maintaining reliable triggering and sufficient mechanical force.

My approach

I broke the problem into independent tests: board connection, digital input behavior, servo power, trigger switching, mechanical actuation, and wiring integrity. This made it easier to isolate failures instead of debugging the entire device at once.

Engineering & iteration

The build required multiple rounds of troubleshooting around switch wiring, connectors, grounding, board behavior, servo selection, and physical packaging. Each test reduced uncertainty before the next integration step.

Result

The work established a practical architecture for a compact electromechanical wearable and a repeatable bench-testing process for refining it.

What I learned

I learned how valuable modular testing is in hardware development. Separating electrical, firmware, and mechanical problems prevents a single failure from obscuring everything else.
