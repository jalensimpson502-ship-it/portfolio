The Challenge

The project required a repeatable process for conducting paint trials while controlling variables that could affect surface quality, material behavior, and test credibility.

My Approach

I supported robot setup and operation, paint preparation, viscosity control, hardware changes, contamination prevention, and visual quality assessment. I treated the work as both a technical process and a controlled experiment. I provided potential kaizen opportunities for process improvement as well as documented the process for future reference.

Engineering & Iteration

Because small changes in setup could affect the result, the work depended on disciplined trial execution, careful inspection, and rapid adjustment. Each trial became a feedback loop for refining process assumptions and test readiness.

Result

The project produced usable technical findings and a structured report that could be communicated beyond the immediate trial team, including leadership and engineering stakeholders.
