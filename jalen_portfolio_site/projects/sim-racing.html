The Challenge

This project aimed to create an immersive motor racing experience that would engage users through realistic force feedback, visual immersion, and haptic responses.

My Approach

I started with understanding what makes an immersive racing experience compelling and then designed the hardware and software to deliver those sensations. Starting with each of the human senses, with a primary focus on feel and perception.

Engineering & Iteration

The build process begin with just a standard wheel and pedal set to understand the basics of force feedback and it's connection to the software. From there I analyzed and recorded behavior from my vehicle in real life to gain an understanding of how to replicate that experience virtually. With each iteration, I designed with the intent of blurring the line between virtual and real-world experiences. The next iterations will focus more on what can be felt while driving in real life such as wind simulation, full dynamic lighting, and actual vehicle dynamics.

Result

So far, the project has yielded a functional immersive experience that effectively delivers realistic force feedback and haptic responses. The integration of various technologies has created a compelling simulation that bridges the gap between virtual and real-world driving experiences. User feedback has been positive, with some suggestions to implement additional features like virtual reality integration that will be investigated further.

What I Learned

I've learned the real importance of the amount of work it takes to become a subject matter expert to create an accurate immersive experience. Even as a hobby, this project required a significant time investment and dedication to understanding the complexities of different systems and their interactions. Above that, understanding the way that the racing simulation works and how to translate real-world physics into a digital environment is crucial for creating a believable experience.
