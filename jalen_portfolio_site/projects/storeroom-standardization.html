The challenge

Multiple facilities had different layouts and operating conditions, making it difficult to compare storage practices and identify a common standard.

My approach

I gathered field measurements, built AutoCAD maps, compared layouts and operations, and organized the results so differences between sites could be communicated clearly.

Engineering & iteration

Because each facility had unique constraints, the work required separating local exceptions from opportunities that could reasonably be standardized across the network.

Result

The project produced a cross-site view of the current state and recommendations that could be communicated to engineering and leadership stakeholders.
