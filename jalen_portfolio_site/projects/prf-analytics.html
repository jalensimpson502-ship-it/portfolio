Collaboration Plant Engineering Planning Group · Study Owners · Leadership · Data Users
01

The Problem

What needed to be solved?

The raw data was not ready to visualize. Inconsistent status definitions, duplicate fiscal-year labels, missing dates, outliers, and multiple interpretations of lateness meant that a polished dashboard could still tell the wrong story.

02

My Approach

How I attacked it.

I started by defining a proper business logic, then built the measures and visuals around those definitions. As edge cases surfaced, I iterated on the model instead of forcing the data to fit the original dashboard design.

03

Impact

Why the work mattered.

The finished workflow made open workload, completion performance, lateness, and data-quality gaps easier to understand while reducing the need to manually interpret raw records.
