About

Engineering with a creative mindset.

I’m a mechanical engineer who enjoys working where physical systems, automation, software, visualization, and human experience overlap. My favorite projects are the ones that require learning fast, prototyping thoughtfully, and explaining complex work clearly.

I’m especially interested in roles where technical rigor and immersive consumer experiences matter at the same time. Moreso, places where engineering supports experiences people can actually feel.
